My Story

How I Got Here

I am a dedicated Digital Marketing Manager and Social Media Manager with a strong passion for helping brands grow through strategic creativity and data-driven decision-making. My background combines a Bachelor’s degree in Visual Communication with postgraduate studies in Project Management and Computer Game Programming—an unusual mix that strengthens my ability to think both analytically and visually.Over the years, I have developed expertise in crafting cohesive digital strategies, managing social media ecosystems, and executing campaigns that enhance brand visibility and engagement. I excel at translating complex ideas into compelling content, optimizing performance through continuous analysis, and coordinating projects from concept to completion.

My multidisciplinary foundation allows me to approach digital marketing with a unique perspective, blending design sensibility, technical understanding, and strategic planning. I thrive in dynamic environments, enjoy solving challenges with creative solutions, and am committed to delivering impactful results for every project I take on.If you are looking for someone who combines creativity, technical insight, and a strong strategic mindset, I would be excited to collaborate with you.
